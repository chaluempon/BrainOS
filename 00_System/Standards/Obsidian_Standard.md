# Obsidian Standard

`01_Knowledge` is the Obsidian vault root.

- Keep `.obsidian` configuration minimal and portable.
- Do not migrate machine-specific `workspace.json`.
- Prefer core plugins; community plugins require documented need and review.
- Use stable filenames and aliases to preserve readable links.
- Index notes are navigation aids, not duplicate canonical content.
- Validate unresolved links, duplicate titles, encoding, and attachments before cutover.
