# AI Prediction Technology

AI-driven prediction has evolved from single-model statistical forecasting to **multi-agent simulation** of entire scenarios — a paradigm shift exemplified by [[MiroFish]].

## Overview

Traditional prediction (regression, time-series, single LLM) is limited: it predicts **outputs** but not **emergent behaviors**. Modern multi-agent approaches simulate **the system that produces outcomes**, enabling richer, more explainable predictions.

## Paradigms

| Paradigm | Example | Limitation | Strength |
|----------|---------|------------|----------|
| Statistical | ARIMA, regression | No agent-level reasoning | Fast, explainable |
| Single LLM | GPT-4 forecast prompts | Hallucination, no dynamics | General knowledge |
| **Multi-agent simulation** | [[MiroFish]] | Compute-intensive | Emergent realism |

## Key Points

- Multi-agent simulation predicts by **rehearsing** in a [[Digital Twin]]
- Outcomes emerge from agent interactions, not hard-coded rules
- Enables **zero-risk** scenario testing (policies, PR, finance)
- Both macro (decision-maker) and micro (creative) use cases
- Depends on [[Swarm Intelligence]] as theoretical foundation
