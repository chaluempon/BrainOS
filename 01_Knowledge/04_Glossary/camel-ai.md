# CAMEL-AI

**CAMEL-AI** is an open-source AI research team/community focused on multi-agent systems and communication. They created the [[OASIS Framework]] that powers [[MiroFish]].

## Definition

An open-source research organization producing frameworks for multi-agent LLM systems. Their flagship work OASIS enables large-scale social interaction simulations.

## Key Facts

- **GitHub:** https://github.com/camel-ai
- **Flagship project:** [[OASIS Framework]]
- **Focus areas:** Multi-agent communication, emergent behavior, LLM orchestration

## Relationship to MiroFish

CAMEL-AI's OASIS is the simulation backbone of MiroFish. MiroFish team publicly acknowledges this dependency.
