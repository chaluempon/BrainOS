# Swarm Intelligence

**Swarm Intelligence** is the collective behavior that emerges from decentralized, self-organized systems — typically made up of many simple agents interacting with each other and their environment.

## Definition

A paradigm where intelligence arises not from a single centralized mind, but from the **collective emergence** triggered by interactions among many individual agents. Used in AI to simulate complex social, economic, or natural systems by modeling many autonomous agents rather than programming explicit rules.

## Key Facts

- Emergent intelligence > individual intelligence
- Used in prediction, optimization, simulation
- Applied in [[MiroFish]] to predict real-world events via agent-society simulation
- Breaks limitations of traditional rule-based or single-model prediction

## How It's Used in MiroFish

MiroFish applies swarm intelligence to create "mirrors that map reality":
- Thousands of agents with **independent personalities**
- Each has **long-term memory** and **behavioral logic**
- Agents **freely interact** and undergo **social evolution**
- Collective outcomes predict real-world trajectories
