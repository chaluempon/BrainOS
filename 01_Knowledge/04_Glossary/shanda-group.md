# Shanda Group

**Shanda Group** (盛大集团) is a Chinese investment holding company providing strategic support and incubation for [[MiroFish]].

## Definition

A major Chinese conglomerate historically known for online gaming and now active in AI, biotech, and technology investments. They are the strategic incubator behind MiroFish.

- **Role in MiroFish:** Strategic support + incubation
