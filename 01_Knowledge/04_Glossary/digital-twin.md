# Digital Twin

A **Digital Twin** (or **Parallel Digital World**) is a high-fidelity virtual replica of a real-world system or environment used to simulate, predict, and test scenarios safely.

## Definition

In [[MiroFish]], the digital twin is a **parallel digital world** populated by thousands of AI agents that mirror the social dynamics of reality. Variables can be injected from a "God's-eye view" to test how changes propagate, allowing prediction of future outcomes without real-world risk.

## Key Facts

- Constructed automatically from seed information
- Populated by AI agents with personalities & memory
- Enables **zero-risk** policy/PR testing
- Supports **dynamic variable injection**
- Used to "rehearse the future"

## Role in MiroFish

- Built in workflow step 1 (Graph Building via [[GraphRAG]])
- Populated in step 2 (persona generation)
- Simulated in step 3 (dual-platform parallel simulation)
- Users can **chat with any agent** in the twin post-simulation
