# Knowledge-to-Platform Contract

**Status:** Draft; runtime implementation not authorized

The future contract must define: approved source delivery (read-only mount, checkout, or release bundle), least privilege, indexable statuses, knowledge release ID, access-level filtering before retrieval and cloud escalation, chunk-to-note/source citations, rebuild and invalidation, backup, and the prohibition on automatic task-memory promotion.

BrainOS_Platform may never write directly to canonical knowledge through the RAG connector.
