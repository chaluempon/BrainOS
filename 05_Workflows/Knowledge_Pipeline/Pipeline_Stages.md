# Pipeline Stages

1. **Ingest:** Record source and custody.
2. **Identify:** Capture author, publisher, date, version, license, and language.
3. **Deduplicate:** Find exact, near, updated, canonical, and source-record matches.
4. **Validate:** Prefer authoritative and current sources.
5. **Research and fact-check:** Compare claims and preserve conflicts.
6. **Extract and classify:** Assign domain, type, status, and access level.
7. **Generate and link:** Follow Markdown/metadata standards and canonical links.
8. **Review:** Check facts, sources, completeness, duplication, security, and links.
9. **Approve and publish:** Human-accountable status transition.
10. **Index:** Update topic, tag, source, and status indexes.
